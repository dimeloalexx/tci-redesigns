The ride, start to finish

Allow two to two and a half hours for the whole thing. Bring sunscreen, water and your phone: the guides will take the pictures, and they lend waterproof cases.

Shoes and socks to borrow if yours won't do, and helmets, which are required under 18. A safety talk, then a quick lesson on your horse while the tack is adjusted.

Down the quiet roads of Long Bay Hills and through a jungly path to the beach. Beginners usually get to trot here. Nobody rides nose to tail.

The long, quiet white beach on the south side of Provo, with calm, shallow water. At low tide the sand is firm, and riders who are ready can canter.

Our horses love the water. Long Bay is never more than about five feet deep; most horses keep their feet down, and experienced riders can look for a deeper spot to swim.

A treat and a cuddle for your horse, and maybe a Provo Ponies T-shirt or hat before you head back to your hotel or villa.

Provo Ponies began as a rescue, not a business. Camille Slattery and a few friends bought seven Paso Finos that had been shipped to Provo from the Dominican Republic and left hungry, thirsty and without shade. Once they were healthy they were far too young to retire, so they went to work, and the rides began. On an island with no grass, every bale of hay is imported, and the team trims every hoof themselves: there is no farrier and no horse vet in the country.

Before you ride

Do I need to book ahead?

Yes. From December to May the rides are sometimes booked two to three months ahead, so write as soon as you know your dates.

I've never ridden. Will I be OK?

Absolutely. The horses are calm and well trained, and the guides take first-time riders all the time. Beginners walk, trot a little on the way down and ride into the water like everyone else.

What should I wear?

Long, light pants and closed shoes are best; sandals with straps, sneakers, reef runners and dive booties are fine. You will get wet. No bare feet and no flip-flops, but shoes and socks can be borrowed.

Who can ride?

Anyone aged 7 and up, up to 215 lb. Children under 12 ride with an adult, and helmets are required under 18. There's no double riding, and nobody rides pregnant or under the influence.

What should I bring?

A card or cash (nothing is charged in advance), sunscreen, water, your phone or camera, any medication you need, and a tip for your guide: 20% is standard for excursions.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancelling the day before costs 50% of the ride. On the day, or if you don't show up, it's the full price.

Write with your dates, the ride you'd like, and each rider's weight and level.
