At Chinson's Grill Shack the jerk and BBQ slow-cook for hours, the wok runs a full Chinese menu, and the bread is baked out back. One shack on Leeward Highway, Providenciales.

Since about 2001

“Even Jamaicans who come here say it tastes authentic.”

Allan Hutchinson, owner

Chinson's opened to cook for the island's growing Jamaican community, and the kitchen kept testing until the jerk, the oxtail and the curry tasted the way they do back home.

It is a colourful shack with a big wooden deck, picnic tables under umbrellas and a bar at one end. The bakery out back sends its bread to other kitchens on the island.
