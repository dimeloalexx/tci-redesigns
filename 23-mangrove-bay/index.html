On the creek

Mangrove Bay is named for the red mangroves along Cheshire Hall Creek, right outside. The tables out back sit on the water, and the breeze comes in off the Caicos Banks.

It isn't on a beach. It's on Industrial Drive in Discovery Bay, between Turk's Head Brewery and the South Dock roundabout, just east of the airport runway.

So now and then a plane lifts off over the creek while you eat.

Inside there's a pool table and a dartboard. Happy hour is 5 to 7 pm on every day they're open.
