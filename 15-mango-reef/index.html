From the kitchen

Mango Reef sits on the water at Turtle Cove Marina, five minutes from Grace Bay, with tables inside, outside and on a covered patio over the cove.

The menu says it plainly: fresh local flavors and seafood, with a selection of international favorites. Local snapper comes off the grill whole or filleted, lobster arrives when the season opens, and oxtail and jerk chicken sit next to them.

Chef Florent Sourmont's kitchen also cooks a dozen escargots, a caramelized apple tarte fine and a raspberry macaron, then crosses to Indonesia for ikan curry and mee goreng, and back to the islands for a Bambarra rum cake.

The ice cream is made here: vanilla, rum and raisin, strawberry, chocolate, pistachio, coconut, and a mango sherbet.
