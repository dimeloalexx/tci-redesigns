From textiles to the Caribbean

Lucie studied Constructed Textiles, then set off to travel the world. She stopped in the Turks and Caicos, fell for the clear water and the bold colours, and decided this was home.

Shown around the world

Her work has been exhibited in the USA, Spain, England and New Zealand, and hangs in private collections and galleries on the island. You can feel the textures in it: try touching one.
