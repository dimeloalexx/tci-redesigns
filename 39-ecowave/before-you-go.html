Before you go

The questions families ask most, and how cancellations, weather and new dates work.

Can children join the charter?
Yes. Share your children's ages when you enquire so we can help you choose activities for the family.
What should we bring?
Pack swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a dry change of clothes. A light layer can come in handy after sunset.
Can we bring our own food or drinks?
Water, sodas, and light snacks are provided. Please check with us before bringing anything extra, and let us know about any food allergies when you enquire.
Where do we meet Sparkles?
Your booking confirmation will include your meeting point and departure time.

Cancellations & weather

If your plans change, please contact us as soon as possible. Guest cancellations follow the policy below.

Your captain will assess the weather and sea conditions before departure. A passing shower may call for a change to the route or start time. If EcoWave cancels the charter due to conditions, we'll contact you straight away about your booking.

Cancellation policy
When we receive your cancellationCancellation fee
At least 48 hours before departureNo fee. All payments, including your deposit, are refunded.
At least 24 hours but less than 48 hours before departure50% of the total charter price
Less than 24 hours before departure, or if you do not arrive100% of the total charter price

Rescheduling

If you contact us at least 48 hours before departure, you may move your booking to another available date once without a change fee. Requests made with less notice are subject to the cancellation terms above.
