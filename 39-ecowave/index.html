Catch a wake, dive in for a snorkel, or watch the sun go down from the deck. With Sparkles reserved for your family or group, you can spend your time on the water doing what everyone came for.

Activities

In Providenciales the sun sets at about 5 p.m. in winter and about 7:30 p.m. in summer.

  • Take turns riding the wake behind Sparkles.

  • Slip beneath the surface and look for colorful reef fish.

  • Cruise between nearby islands and cays, with time for photos or a swim.

Meet Sparkles.

Sparkles is EcoWave's turquoise 2019 Axis T23 inboard wake boat. Up to 12 people on board, including crew.

Grab a board and take your turn behind the boat while your family cheers you on. Then cool off with a swim or settle in for a cruise between the cays. When someone calls for one more run, you're right where you need to be.
