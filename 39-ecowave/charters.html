Choose your private charter

Routes and activities depend on your group's preferences, charter length, and the day's weather and sea conditions.

Half-Day Charter

3 hours

Make a morning or afternoon of it. Pick the activities your group is most excited about, from a few wakeboard runs to a snorkel stop and a swim.

  • Wakeboarding
  • Snorkeling
  • Island hopping to nearby cays
  • Swimming and coastal cruising
Enquire about a half day

Full-Day Charter

6 hours

Give everyone time for their favorite activity. Start with a wakeboarding session, stop to snorkel, then spend the rest of the charter exploring the cays, swimming, or relaxing aboard Sparkles.

  • Wakeboarding
  • Snorkeling
  • Island hopping with more time to explore
  • Swimming and relaxing on board
Enquire about a full day

Private Sunset Cruise

Late afternoon

Head out in the late afternoon and watch the coastline turn golden. Settle in with a cold soda and enjoy the last light of the day with your favorite people.

Enquire about a sunset cruise

What's included

  • Private use of Sparkles throughout your charter
  • An itinerary planned around your group's preferred activities
  • Water, soft drinks (sodas), and light snacks served on board
