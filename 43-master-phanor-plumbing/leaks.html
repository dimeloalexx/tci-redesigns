Do this first

  1. Shut off the water. Close the main valve, usually near the meter or where the pipe comes into the house. If your water comes from a tank, switch off the pump.
  2. Switch off the water heater if the leak is near it or the heater itself is leaking.
  3. Keep water away from electricity. If water is near sockets or appliances, switch off the power at the breaker. If you are not sure it is safe, stay out of the water.
  4. Catch what you can with buckets and towels, and lift things off the floor.
  5. Take a photo or a short video of where the water is coming from.
  6. Call or WhatsApp us and send the photo, so we come with the right parts.

Find a hidden leak

Some leaks never show a drop. These checks tell you if water is escaping somewhere.

The toilet colour test

Put a few drops of food colouring in the toilet tank and do not flush. If colour shows up in the bowl after 10 minutes, the toilet is leaking.

The test is from the US EPA's WaterSense programme.

  • Watch the meter star

    Close every tap and look at your water meter. If the small star or wheel is turning, water is leaking somewhere. See what a leak wastes

  • Read the meter twice

    Write down the numbers, use no water for two hours, then read it again. If the numbers moved, there is a leak (US EPA).

  • Listen to the pump

    If your house runs on a tank and pump, a pump that switches on by itself while every tap is closed is usually pushing water out through a leak.
