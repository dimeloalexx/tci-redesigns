Most of Provo's water starts in the sea

About nine of every ten gallons used on Providenciales come from a plant that takes the salt out of sea water. A leak lets that water run away, day and night.

From Times of the Islands, 2025: the island's water then travels through 170 miles of pipe to the taps.

Is your meter's star spinning?

Most water meters have a small star or wheel that turns when water flows. Close every tap in the house and look at it. If it still turns, water is leaking somewhere.

Pick a leak to see what it wastes
