Plain door, full plates.

Fifteen years in the plaza

From outside it's easy to walk past: a shopfront in Williams Plaza, a few steps from the government offices and the post office. Inside it's small and warm, and it has fed downtown for more than fifteen years.

The patio after dark

Out front, umbrellas and a wooden deck. Evenings are slow and easy: plates of jerk, a cold beer, dominoes on the table. Locals come straight from work; travellers come with their bags.
