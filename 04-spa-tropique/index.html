1997

The island's longest-running spa.

Spa Tropique has been pampering guests on Providenciales since 1997. Owner Meryl Cooper, a New Yorker who made the island home, built it around one idea: it's all about you.

  • Friendly, talented therapists and estheticians
  • A licensed physical therapist on call
  • Weddings, spa parties and groups, creatively flexible
  • Gift certificates, and a great wedding registry gift

The blah, blah, blah…

Reservations

Pre-bookings are accepted and appreciated. A credit card is required to secure your reservation.

Cancellations

24 hours' notice is required to cancel or reschedule. No-shows are charged in full.

Running late?

Late arrivals may mean a shorter treatment so the next guest can start on time. Full value of the service applies.

Mobile spa

Everything on the menu can come to you except hot stone massage. A $50 outcall fee applies per therapist, not per service.

Gratuities & tax

Gratuities are not included and are at your discretion. Prices are in USD and do not include the 12% government tax. Resort fees may apply.

Mommy-to-be

Our prenatal massage ("Oh 'Baby', You Need This!") is for the 2nd and 3rd trimester: 60 minutes, $170.
