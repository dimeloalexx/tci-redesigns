Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in, or call 649 946 4367 to book a time.

When are you open?

Weekdays from 9 am to 5 pm. Call before a public holiday to check.

Can I have my blood test here?

Yes, the lab is part of the practice. Some tests need you to fast first; the slip above works out when to stop eating.

Can you fill my prescription?

Yes. The pharmacy is in the same practice, so you can collect your medicine before you leave.

Where do I park?

There's plenty of free parking all around Town Centre Mall.

What if it's an emergency?

For anything life-threatening, call 911. For an urgent problem during opening hours, come in or call us first.
