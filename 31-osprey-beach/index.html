    The Wall

    Starts at 25 to 30 feet

    Grand Turk's reef wall drops into the deep a quarter to half a mile offshore, with visibility often past 100 feet. All three dive shops pick up on the beach by the hotel.

    Before you fly

    How do I get to Grand Turk?

    Fly into Providenciales (PLS), then take the 30-minute flight to Grand Turk (GDT) with interCaribbean or Caicos Express. A taxi to the hotel takes about 10 minutes, around $10 a person.

    Do the dive boats really pick up at the hotel?

    Yes. Blue Water Divers is on the property and picks up on the Osprey's beach, Grand Turk Diving picks up on the beach next door, and Oasis Divers is a three or four minute walk along Duke Street. Groups of 10 or more can ask for dive resort rates.

    Where can we eat?

    The Birdcage serves breakfast, lunch and dinner by the pool, with BBQ nights on Monday, Wednesday and Saturday. Also on site: Cakeos Cafe for coffee and breakfast (no credit cards), and the Fisherman's Wife with Chef Jureka for lunch and dinner.

    What is the cancellation policy?

    Cancel more than 30 days before arrival and any deposit is refunded less a $35 processing fee. Less than 30 days before, there is no refund.

    Can we get married here?

    Yes. The hotel arranges relaxed beach weddings and vow renewals, with a minister or Justice of the Peace, and a reception by the pool.

    Is there a spa?

    Therapeutic massage and body treatments by appointment: call 649 242 9746.
