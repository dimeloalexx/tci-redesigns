Top O'the Cove

New York style deli on Leeward Highway, feeding Turks and Caicos for more than twenty years.

Open daily from 7 am

Mon to Fri 7 to 5 · Sat 7 to 3 · Sun 7 to 2

Angela's deli at Suzy Turn: bread baked every morning, pastrami piled on rye, New York bagels, pizza by the slice and coffee people cross the island for. Eat in the cool or out on the patio.

Who. Angela and her team, friendly and attentive. Locals just call the place Angela's, and plenty of them come in only for a fresh roll and butter.

What. Fresh bread, pastries, gourmet coffee, deli meats and cheeses, pizza, soups, desserts and cookies, with gluten free products too. Smoothies, juice, beer and wine, and a deli shop of gourmet foods, chocolates and seasonal gifts.

Where. 1059B Leeward Highway at Suzy Turn, across from the hill down to Turtle Cove and right next to Bayview Motors. Air-conditioned inside, a patio outside, and Wi-Fi if you need to get some work done.

When. Monday to Friday 7 am to 5 pm, Saturday 7 to 3 and Sunday 7 to 2, with breakfast all day on Sundays. No booking needed; call 649 946 4694.

Before you pull in

What are the hours?

Monday to Friday 7 am to 5 pm, Saturday 7 am to 3 pm, Sunday 7 am to 2 pm.

Can I get breakfast at lunchtime?

On Sundays, yes: breakfast is served all day. The rest of the week it runs alongside lunch from 7 am.

Is there Wi-Fi and somewhere to sit?

Yes. There's air-conditioned seating inside, a patio outside, and Wi-Fi for anyone who needs to get some work done over a coffee.

Do I need to book?

No booking needed. Walk in, or call ahead on 649 946 4694 if you want your order ready.

Is there anything gluten free?

Gluten free products are available. Ask at the counter for what's in today.

Do you cater?

Yes, for any occasion, from fruit and cheese platters to a spread of sandwiches. Angela is the one to call, or use the form below.

Can I have a beer with lunch?

Yes, beer and wine are on the menu, along with smoothies, juices and soft drinks.

Where exactly is it?

1059B Leeward Highway at Suzy Turn, across from the hill that leads down to Turtle Cove, right next to Bayview Motors. It's easy to drive past, so look for it next to the car dealer.

Feeding a crowd?

Catering for any occasion: platters of fruit and cheese, sandwiches and more.
