3 Where to?

  • Grace Bay →

    The famous beach, its resorts and restaurants.

  • Chalk Sound →

    A turquoise lagoon dotted with tiny cays, and Sapodilla Hill just above it.

  • Turtle Cove →

    Marina restaurants and the reef right off the beach.

  • Cheshire Hall →

    Ruins of a Loyalist cotton plantation from the 1790s.

  • The Bight Fish Fry →

    Thursday nights, local food and music by the beach.

  • Anywhere else →

    Supermarket, ferry, doctor, a friend's villa. Just ask.
