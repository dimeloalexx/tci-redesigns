Before you come

  • Coco Bistro palm grove, Grace Bay Road
  • Every day, 11 am to 9 pm
  • Takeout orders: 649 231 8226
Do I need a reservation?

No. Walk up to the window, order, and take a picnic table under the palms.

Can I order ahead for takeout?

Yes. Call 649 231 8226 with your order and pick it up at the Airstream.

What gets added to the bill?

All prices have 12% government tax and a 10% service charge added.

Is there food for vegetarians?

The Beyond Veggie Burger, the Spicy Falafel Bowl, Buffalo Cauliflower and the salads. The mahi tacos also come in a vegetarian version.

And for kids?

A kids hot dog or a chicken or steak quesadilla, both with fries, plus milkshakes, ice cream sandwiches and scoops.

Anything about allergies?

Every sandwich comes on a homemade, sesame-free bun. Tell the window about any other allergy before you order.

Where do I park?

There is free parking on site, shared with Coco Bistro.
