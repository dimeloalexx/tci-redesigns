About The Artist

Hi, my name is Jasmine, a professional makeup artist with over 8 years of experience helping women feel confident, beautiful, and camera-ready for their most important moments. Whether it's a wedding, special event, or photoshoot, I take pride in creating gorgeous personalized looks that enhance your natural features.

I truly love what I do, and I'm always learning new techniques to stay inspired and bring fresh, modern artistry to each client I work with. For me, it's not just about makeup. It's about making you feel like the best version of yourself.
