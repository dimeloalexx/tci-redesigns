Booking Policies

Deposit

A deposit of $40 + $3 PayPal fee is required to secure your appointment. The remaining balance must be paid in cash only.

Cancellation

A 24-hour notice is required to cancel or reschedule any service.

Extra fees

Appointments before 9 AM, after 7 PM, on Sundays, or booked on the same day are subject to an additional fee.

Tardiness

A $25 fee will be added to your balance if you are 10 minutes late. After 15 minutes, your appointment may be cancelled.

Good to know

  • For safety and sanitization, no children or extra guests are allowed unless being serviced.
  • Groom your brows, arrive on time and come with no makeup.
