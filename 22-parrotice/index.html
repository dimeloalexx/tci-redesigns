Eight flavours, churned right here every single day, on the one road through North Caicos.

Patti DesLauriers opened Parrotice in July 2019 on land she and Howie Bartels had owned since 2002. Coconut is the best seller.

Eight flavours, made here: coconut, vanilla, chocolate, strawberry, rum raisin, peppermint, cookies and cream, butter pecan, and one more that changes every week.

Every flavour comes in a cone or a cup. The flavour of the week is written up at the window, so ask what it is before you choose.

The famous two

Coconut is the one everybody orders. Rum raisin is the grown-up one, made with Bambarra, the islands' own rum. If you can't decide, a cup holds both.

Built by friends

The shop as the people who made it in 2019, from the story in Times of the Islands.

The idea

Patti DesLauriers

Owner

Cleared the land in 2018, tested recipe after recipe on friends and neighbours, and painted the shop herself in colours you can see from the road.

“I always thought this island needed an ice cream shop.”

Patti DesLauriers, Times of the Islands, 2019

Recipes

Sherry Williams

Manager when the shop opened

Made the ice cream and helped Patti get the recipes right, down to the banana made with fruit grown on the island.

Building

Charlton Gardiner

Builder, Major Hill

Put up the building itself, the little shop that now sits among the palms on King's Highway.

Tables

Howie Bartels

Carpenter

Added the porch, finished the inside and made the tables and outdoor seats where everyone eats.

Jim Frey

Wood carver

Carved Iceman, the shop's parrot, out of casuarina wood.
