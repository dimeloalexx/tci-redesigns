Meet Chef Romane

I started cooking at seven, next to my grandmother in Jamaica. Cooking is still my happy place.

I trained and got certified at Beaches and Riu resorts in Jamaica, and I have 16 years in food and beverage. I'm a bartender and a butler too, so your evening is looked after from the first drink to the last plate.

I cook food from around the world: Jamaican and Caribbean, seafood, Italian, Japanese and fusion. I speak English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian, so everyone at your table can talk to me in their own language.

My promise is simple: a perfect experience, or your money back.
