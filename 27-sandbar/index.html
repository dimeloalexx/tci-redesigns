    The Sandbar Restaurant sits right on the water just steps from Manta House. The quintessential beach bar… no shoes or shirt required.

    We serve a variety of local seafood specialties, including fresh caught grouper, lobster, conch, wahoo and mahi mahi. We also serve North American fare like our famous Sandbar burgers, shrimp quesadillas, chicken and ribs with island style peas and rice.

    Good to know

    Are you open on Saturdays? No. The Sandbar opens Sunday to Friday and rests on Saturday.

    When does the kitchen close? Food orders stop at 3 pm. The bar keeps pouring until sunset.

    Do I need to dress up? No shoes or shirt required. Come straight off the beach or out of the water.

    Can I swim right there? Yes. The bar sits on the calm beach on Grand Turk's west coast, with beach chairs on the sand.

    I'm on a cruise. Is it far? Cockburn Town is a short taxi ride north of the cruise centre. Use Ship time above to work out when to head back.

    Is there something for vegetarians? Salads, baked potatoes, peas and rice and cheese quesadillas.

    Will I see whales? Humpbacks pass Grand Turk in winter, roughly January to April. Keep an eye on the water while you eat.

    What's the green flash? A brief green spark on the horizon the moment the sun disappears, on a clear evening. The bar's open until then.

    Can we hold a party or a wedding here? Yes, the Sandbar hosts private beach events and beach-themed weddings. Use the coupon below.

    Beach party or wedding?

    Tell the Sandbar the date and how many are coming. Your email app opens with it all written.
