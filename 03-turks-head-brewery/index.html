Five beers, five island sayings.

Every Turk's Head is named for something people here really say. Learn all five and you're half a local.

I-Soon-Reach

Light lager · our best-seller · award-winning

“I'm nearly there.” Said with total confidence by someone who hasn't left the house yet.

Pale, crisp and light on its feet. The beer for a long afternoon in the heat when you have nowhere to be, soon.

I-Ain-Ga-Lie

Lager

“I'm not going to lie to you.” The phrase right before the truth, or right before a story.

A clean golden lager with a little more malt behind it. Honest, like it says on the can.

Down-Da-Road

IPA · award-winning

“Just down the road.” Island directions. Could be two minutes, could be the other end of Provo.

Bright hops up front and a dry, bitter finish. The one for people who like their beer to say something.

Gon-Ta-Nort

Amber ale

“Gone to North.” As in North Caicos: a ferry ride away, quieter, greener, slower.

Copper-coloured and toasty, with caramel malt and a smooth finish. Easy company.

Gon-Ta-Sout

Stout

“Gone to South.” South Caicos, the far end of the chain, where the night sky is darkest.

Dark and roasty with a creamy tan head. Proof that a stout works at 30°C.

The cactus in the red hat.

The Turk's head cactus grows wild along the dry, rocky coasts of these islands. Its red cap looks like a fez, which gave it the name. It's on the national coat of arms, and it's the name on every can we've filled since 2001.
