Coming as a couple, with friends or as a family? Share the kayak and the shoot: ask us for a group price.

Good to know

No paddling

The water is 2 to 3.5 feet deep. You lie back, sit or pose; the kayak stays put.

Never alone

Your photographer stays in the water beside you, helping with poses and keeping you comfortable.

Where we meet

Sunset Beach in Leeward, at the end of Tranquility Lane. Get directions.

When

We are open 8 am to 6 pm. Calm, sunny days give the clearest water.

What to wear and bring

  • Clear deodorant, if you can
  • No heavy oils or lotions: they make you slide while you hold a pose
  • Sunglasses, for the sun and for the look
  • Props you love: a cover-up, a hat, a scarf

Questions

Do I need to know how to kayak?

No. The kayak sits in shallow, calm water and you never paddle. You just float and pose.

Can I bring my partner or friends?

Yes. Couples, friends and families can share the kayak and the shoot. Message us for a group price.

What do I get?

100+ drone photos, 2 drone videos and 10 edited photos from a 30-minute session.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before your session and it is free.

Where do we meet?

On Sunset Beach in Leeward, Providenciales. We send you the exact spot when you book.
