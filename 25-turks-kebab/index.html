    Zemar's hot sauce

    Number 97 on the menu, $3 on the side. Bold island heat that lifts a gyro or a plate of fries without burning out the flavour.

    Take a bottle home for $9. It sits on the shelf next to two other island makers: Don Martin's Turks and Caicos coffee blend and passion fruit liqueur.

    A family recipe from Turks and Caicos, made in small batches with hand-picked Scotch bonnet peppers. It adds heat that doesn't overpower; it just brings the plate up.

    Buy a bottle, $9

    From six seats to Grace Bay.

    Where it started

    In 2010 Zemar, a local mother with a grill and a plan, opened a shack on Airport Road with six seats. She carved the meat off the spit, wrapped it in warm pita and the line kept growing. No grand opening, no investors.

    Who runs it now

    It is still a family place. Zemar's sons Travis and Mario work beside her, and Travis runs the floor, the operations and the Instagram. The hot sauce on every table is hers.

    Before you come

    When are you open?

    Monday to Saturday, 11 am to 11 pm. Sundays the kitchen is closed.

    Do I need a reservation?

    Only for groups of ten or more. Everyone else can just come by. For a big group, send the request below for 8, 9, 10 or 11 pm.

    Can I order ahead for pickup?

    Yes. Build your order by number above and call 431-9964. When the tables are full at the busiest times, pickup is the easy way.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, through the Order Up delivery service. Find Turks Kebab in their app or on discover.orderup.cc.

    Is there seating inside?

    All the seating is outdoors, in the open air at Grace Bay Market. At peak dinner times it can fill up.

    What can vegetarians eat?

    Plenty: the falafel wrap, the Mediterranean and hummus pitas, all the dips, the falafels, the Greek and falafel salads and most of the sides. They're marked on the menu.

    Where do I park?

    There is free parking at Grace Bay Market. From Ports of Call, Seven Stars or the Ritz-Carlton it's less than five minutes on foot.

    Can I come in a wheelchair?

    Yes. The restaurant is at ground level with only a small lip at the entrance, and the dining area and restrooms are accessible. Tell the staff if you need anything.

    Ten or more of you?

    Reservations are only needed for big groups. Send the request and the family will get back to you.
