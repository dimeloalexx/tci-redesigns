Nancy loves to push people to their limits, and herself first. Working at a college where police officers, firefighters and paramedics did their physical training, she saw how far people can go past what they think is their limit. She has brought that intensity to every session at Glow ever since.

She is an ISSA Elite Personal Trainer, a corrective exercise specialist and a Precision Nutrition coach, because, as she puts it, “you will never out-train a bad diet.” Sneaky Pete , the Boston terrier, coached the lockdown workouts from the sofa.
