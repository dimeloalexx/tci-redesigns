Tribe is a conscious kitchen. Everything is made fresh every day, with local and sustainable ingredients.

The sourdough is ours, baked in-house, and so are the croissants, cinnamon rolls, cookies and muffins that come out each morning.

Come up the stairs at Ports of Call, take a table looking out over the coconut palms, and stay as long as you like.

The Wi-Fi password is JOINTHETRIBE.
